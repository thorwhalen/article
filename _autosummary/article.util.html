article.util#

Thin, dependency-light utilities shared across the package.

Nothing here knows about platforms or pipelines — these are general helpers: logging, human-like async pacing (so automating your own account doesn’t trip automated-traffic heuristics), atomic JSON IO for the state store, and the canonical-URL guard that every secondary adapter calls so the SSOT link is never silently dropped.

Side-effecting primitives (sleeping, the clock, the RNG) are injected as keyword-only parameters so the helpers stay deterministic under test.
